---
title: Credits and pricing
description: What each search, story and agent run costs, and when you are not charged.
---

Requests are paid from one balance of **NCC** credits. Only successful work is
charged.

## News API

A search is charged by the [effort](/news/effort-levels) you ask for:

| Request | Credits |
|---|---|
| `/search` with `effort=low` | 1 NCC |
| `/search` with `effort=medium` (default) | 5 NCC |
| `/search` with `effort=high` | 10 NCC |
| `/stories/{story_id}` | 1 NCC, whatever the number of articles |
| [`/articles`](/news/articles) | 5 NCC, whatever the page size or the number of results |
| `/subscription` | Free |

- A search is charged only when it succeeds (`200`). A search that finds
  nothing is still charged.
- A request that is refused or fails (`401`, `402`, `403`, `408`, `422`, `429`,
  `500`, `503`) is never charged.
- Counts over time (`count_articles` in the [MCP server](/integrations/mcp#credits))
  make one 5 NCC `/articles` call per query and time step, up to 60 calls a
  request. A 30-day daily trend for two queries is 60 calls, 300 NCC.

Your plan's prices are in the `credits_per_search`, `story` and `articles`
fields of
[`GET /subscription`](/get-started/rate-limits#check-your-plan).

## Agent

An events run is charged per run, by its mode, and per extra 50 rows past the
first 50. Company research and company monitors are charged per company.

| Run | Credits |
|---|---|
| Event research, `fast` | 1,000 NCC per run, 50 rows included |
| Event research, `standard` | 3,000 NCC per run, 50 rows included |
| Event research, `max` | 6,000 NCC per run, 50 rows included |
| Each extra 50 rows (`fast` / `standard` / `max`) | +500 / +1,500 / +3,000 NCC |
| Company research | 500 NCC per company |
| Event monitor, daily | Half a run of its mode |
| Event monitor, weekly or less often | One run of its mode |
| Company monitor | About 3,000 NCC per company per month, charged per update ([below](#company-monitors)) |

Failed runs are free. A run that finds nothing is still charged.

### Company monitors

A company monitor is charged per company update as it runs, never in advance:
25 NCC for an update every 6 hours, or 100 NCC for a daily one. The service
picks the rhythm from how much news each company gets, so a company comes to
about 3,000 NCC a month either way.

- An update that fails is free.
- If several of your organization's monitors follow the same company, each
  update of that company is charged once.
- Pausing or deleting a monitor stops the charges from its next update.
- A monitor that follows a [company list](/agent/company-lists) picks up added
  companies from its next run and charges for them from then on. Removed
  companies stop costing.

### Free monitor

Each organization has one free monitor. It is your oldest enabled monitor that
is either:

- a company monitor, with any number of companies up to your plan's list limit
  (10 on Free, 50 on pay as you go), or
- a `fast` event monitor that runs daily or weekly.

Its runs and its company updates cost nothing. It pauses itself
(`paused_reason: "idle"`) when its results are not opened for 14 days; resume
it to go on.

## When your balance runs out

A News API request your balance does not cover gets `402`. A monitor whose
runs your balance no longer covers is paused with `paused_reason:
"out_of_credits"`: top up, then resume it. Company monitors pause once the
balance can't cover one daily update (100 NCC).
