Enterprise plans include everything in the public plans, and add the data,
limits and terms below. The Enterprise APIs are documented under
**Enterprise** in the version menu.

## More data on every result

Add these to every `/search` result with `include`:

- **Named entities**: people, organizations, locations and other names, also
  found in the English translation.
- **Sentiment** of the headline and of the article, from -1 to 1.
- **Themes** of the article.
- **Publisher descriptions**: the description the publisher wrote for the
  article.
- **English translations** of the headline and summary of non-English
  articles.
- **Embeddings**: a 1,024-value vector of the article.

See [Response fields](/news/response-fields#enterprise-fields) for the field
names.

## Local news data

The [Local News API](/local-news-api/get-started/introduction) returns
location-specific news, with geographic relevance detection and GeoNames
filtering.

## Every match and the archive

The [v3 News API](/news-api/get-started/introduction) returns every article
that matches a boolean query, with pagination, from 2019 onward.

## Larger company runs

Company runs of more than 50 companies, split into
[batches](/agent/runs#batches) and handled as one run.

## Higher limits

- Up to 100 results per search (50 on the free plan).
- Higher requests per second and higher daily limits.

## Contract terms

A signed Enterprise agreement can include terms the public plans don't, such
as rights to redistribute data, an uptime SLA and volume pricing. Where the
agreement differs from the public terms, the agreement applies.
