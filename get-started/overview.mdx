NewsCatcher gives your application and your AI agents two ways to work with
the news. Both use one API key and one credit balance.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="News API" icon="magnifying-glass" href="/news/quickstart">
    Ask in plain words or keywords and get the best articles of the last 30
    days in a second or two, ranked and grouped into stories.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Agent" icon="robot" href="/agent/quickstart">
    Describe the events you need, or give a list of companies. The agent
    researches in the background and returns a table, once or on a schedule.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Which one do I need?

| You want | Use | Answer in |
|---|---|---|
| The top articles about something, right now | [`/search`](/news/quickstart) | Seconds |
| Every article of one story | [`/stories/{story_id}`](/news/stories) | Seconds |
| A table of events, for example every bank M&A deal since September 15 | [Event research](/agent/event-research) | 1 to 30 minutes |
| The news about each company in a list | [Company research](/agent/company-research) | Minutes |
| New events or company news delivered hourly, daily or weekly | [Monitors](/agent/monitors) | On a schedule |

See [Choose an API](/get-started/choose-an-api) for the full comparison.
