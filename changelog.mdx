<Update label="November 4, 2026" description="News API 1.1.0">
  ## Every matching article with /articles

  - **`/articles`**: `GET` and `POST /articles` return every article that
    matches a boolean query over the last 12 months, newest first, to page
    through: up to 100 per page and the first 1,000 per query, with an exact
    `total_hits`. A successful call costs 5 NCC. See
    [Every matching article](/news/articles).
  - **`/subscription`**: the new `articles` field shows the limits and the
    price of `/articles` on your plan. See
    [Rate limits](/get-started/rate-limits#check-your-plan).
  - **Errors**: `408` means an `/articles` search ran out of time. It is not
    charged. See [Errors](/get-started/errors).
</Update>

<Update label="October 30, 2026" description="Launch">
  ## New News API and Agent

  - **News API**: `/search` ranks the last 30 days of news in every language,
    groups results into stories and summarizes each article, at three
    [effort levels](/news/effort-levels).
  - **Stories**: `/stories/{story_id}` lists every article of one story.
  - **Agent**: research events or companies in the background and get a
    table, once or on a schedule with [monitors](/agent/monitors).
  - The v3 News API, the Web Search API and the Local News API docs move under
    **Enterprise** in the version menu.
</Update>
