---
title: Glossary
description: Terms used across the News API and agent docs.
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Article">
    One news article from one publisher, with its headline, link, publication
    time, language and one-line summary.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Story">
    The group of articles that report the same event. Every search result has
    the `story` it belongs to.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Newsrooms">
    The number of distinct publishers that cover a story.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Effort">
    How much ranking work a search gets: `low`, `medium` or `high`. It sets the
    price of the search.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="NCC">
    NewsCatcher credits, the unit your requests are charged in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Run">
    One piece of agent research, started with `POST /runs`: events from a
    question, or news about companies.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Mode">
    How deep an events run goes: `fast`, `standard` or `max`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Row">
    One line of a run's table: one event, or one story about a company.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Batch">
    A company list too big for one run, split into runs of up to 50 companies
    and handled as one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Company list">
    A saved set of companies, resolved to the businesses they are, that runs
    and monitors can use.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Monitor">
    Research repeated on a schedule that delivers only what is new: a company
    monitor or an event (dataset) monitor.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
