Filters narrow the articles a search ranks. In the JSON body of `POST /search`
list filters are arrays or comma-separated strings; in the query string of
`GET /search` they are comma-separated strings.

## Time window

<ParamField body="window_hours" type="integer" default="24">
  How many hours back to search, from 1 up to your plan's history (720 hours =
  30 days on the free plan). The window applies to the publication date, or to
  the crawl date with `sort_by=crawled_at`. A window longer than your history
  is cut to it, and the response says `"clamped": true` in `window`. Above
  720 the request gets `422`.
</ParamField>

## Languages, countries and publishers

<ParamField body="languages" type="string | string[]">
  Only articles in these languages:
  [ISO 639-1 codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_639-1),
  at most 100, for example `["en", "de"]`. `zh` is searched as `cn`. If none of
  them is English, the search runs in the original language instead of the
  English translation.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="not_languages" type="string | string[]">
  Leave out articles in these languages: ISO 639-1 codes, at most 100. `zh` is
  excluded as `cn`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="countries" type="string | string[]">
  Only articles from publishers in these countries:
  [ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_3166-1_alpha-2),
  at most 100, for example `["US", "GB"]`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="not_countries" type="string | string[]">
  Leave out articles from publishers in these countries: ISO 3166-1 alpha-2
  codes, at most 100.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="sources" type="string | string[]">
  Only these publishers: domains, at most 100, for example
  `["reuters.com", "bbc.co.uk"]`. Matches any copy of an article, and the
  result then shows the domain you asked for: its name and a link on that
  domain.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="not_sources" type="string | string[]">
  Leave out these publishers: domains, at most 100. An article is dropped when
  any of its copies comes from one of them.
</ParamField>

## Coverage and content type

<ParamField body="min_newsrooms" type="integer" default="0">
  Only stories carried by at least this many newsrooms (0 to 1,000). See
  [Stories](/news/stories).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="content_type" type="string" default="news">
  `news` keeps real news articles only. `all` returns every article, whatever
  its type.
</ParamField>

## Order and size of the results

<ParamField body="sort_by" type="string" default="relevancy">
  - `relevancy`: best match first.
  - `published_at`: newest publication first.
  - `crawled_at`: newest crawl first.

  A date order sorts the best matches of `q` (at least 100, or 5 times
  `limit`), not every article of the window. With a date order,
  `collapse=diverse` keeps every article, as `duplicates` does. A publication
  date later than the crawl date counts as the crawl date.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="limit" type="integer" default="20">
  Number of articles to return, 1 to 100. Your plan may allow fewer (50 on the
  free plan); a larger `limit` gets `403`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="collapse" type="string" default="diverse">
  What happens to several articles of one story: `diverse`, `story` or
  `duplicates`. See [Stories](/news/stories#repeated-articles-of-one-story).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="include" type="string | string[]">
  Enterprise fields to add to every result. Other plans get `403` with
  `enterprise_field`. See [Response fields](/news/response-fields#enterprise-fields).
</ParamField>

## Example

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.newscatcherapi.com/news-api/search" \
  -H "x-api-key: $NEWSCATCHER_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "q": "port strike",
    "window_hours": 72,
    "countries": ["US", "CA"],
    "not_sources": ["example-aggregator.com"],
    "min_newsrooms": 3,
    "collapse": "story",
    "sort_by": "published_at",
    "limit": 50
  }'
```
