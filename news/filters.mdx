---
title: Filters
description: Narrow a search by time window, language, country, publisher, coverage and content type.
---

Filters narrow the articles a search ranks. In `GET /search` list filters are
comma-separated strings; in the JSON body of `POST /search` they can also be
arrays.

<ParamField query="window_hours" type="integer" default="24">
  How many hours back to search, from 1 up to your plan's history (720 hours =
  30 days on the free plan). A longer window is cut to your history, and the
  response says `"clamped": true` in `window`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="languages" type="string | string[]">
  Only articles in these languages: ISO 639-1 codes, at most 100, for example
  `en,de`. `zh` is searched as `cn`. If none of them is English, the original
  text is searched instead of the English translation.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="countries" type="string | string[]">
  Only articles from publishers in these countries:[ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_3166-1_alpha-2), at most 100, for example `US,GB`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="sources" type="string | string[]">
  Only these publishers: domains, at most 100, for example
  `reuters.com,bbc.co.uk`. Matches any copy of an article, and the result shows
  the domain you asked for.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="min_newsrooms" type="integer" default="0">
  Only stories carried by at least this many newsrooms (0 to 1,000). See
  [Stories](/news/stories).
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="content_type" type="string" default="news">
  `news` keeps real news articles only. `all` returns every article, whatever
  its type.
</ParamField>

## Shaping the results

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer" default="20">
  Number of articles to return, 1 to 100. Your plan may allow fewer (50 on the
  free plan); a larger `limit` gets `403`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="collapse" type="string" default="diverse">
  What happens to several articles of one story: `diverse`, `story`,
  `duplicates` or `none`. See [Stories](/news/stories#repeated-articles-of-one-story).
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="include" type="string | string[]">
  Enterprise fields to add to every result: `description`, `content`,
  `entities`, `sentiment`, `themes`, `translation`, `embedding`. Other plans get
  `403` with `enterprise_field`. See [Response fields](/news/response-fields#enterprise-fields).
</ParamField>

## Example

```json POST /search
{
  "q": "port strike",
  "window_hours": 72,
  "countries": ["US", "CA"],
  "min_newsrooms": 3,
  "collapse": "story",
  "limit": 50
}
```
