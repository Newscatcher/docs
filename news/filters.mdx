Filters narrow the articles a search ranks. In the JSON body of `POST /search`
list filters are arrays or comma-separated strings; in the query string of
`GET /search` they are comma-separated strings.

<Note>
  [`/articles`](/news/articles) takes the language, country and publisher
  filters of this page, plus its own parameters in
  [Only for `/articles`](#only-for-articles). The other parameters are for
  `/search` only.
</Note>

## Time window

<ParamField body="search_window" type="string" default="7d">
  How far back to search, counted from now: a number and a unit, for example
  `15m`, `2h`, `1d`, `1w`, `15 minutes`, `2 hours` or `1 day`. Units are
  minutes (`m`, `min`, `minute`, `minutes`), hours (`h`, `hr`, `hour`,
  `hours`), days (`d`, `day`, `days`) and weeks (`w`, `week`, `weeks`). From
  1 minute up to your plan's history (30 days on the free plan). The window
  applies to the publication date, or to the crawl date with
  `sort_by=crawled_at`. A window longer than your history is cut to it, and
  the response says `"clamped": true` in `window`.
</ParamField>

## Languages, countries and publishers

<ParamField body="languages" type="string | string[]">
  Only articles in these languages:
  [ISO 639-1 codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_639-1),
  at most 100, for example `["en", "de"]`. `zh` is searched as `cn`. If none of
  them is English, the search runs in the original language instead of the
  English translation.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="not_languages" type="string | string[]">
  Leave out articles in these languages: ISO 639-1 codes, at most 100. `zh` is
  excluded as `cn`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="countries" type="string | string[]">
  Only articles from publishers in these countries:
  [ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_3166-1_alpha-2),
  at most 100, for example `["US", "GB"]`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="not_countries" type="string | string[]">
  Leave out articles from publishers in these countries: ISO 3166-1 alpha-2
  codes, at most 100.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="sources" type="string | string[]">
  Only these publishers: domains, at most 100, for example
  `["reuters.com", "bbc.co.uk"]`. Matches any copy of an article, and the
  result then shows the domain you asked for: its name and a link on that
  domain.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="not_sources" type="string | string[]">
  Leave out these publishers: domains, at most 100. An article is dropped when
  any of its copies comes from one of them.
</ParamField>

## Coverage and content type

<ParamField body="min_newsrooms" type="integer" default="0">
  Only stories carried by at least this many newsrooms (0 to 1,000). See
  [Stories](/news/stories).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="content_type" type="string" default="news">
  `news` keeps real news articles only. `all` returns every article, whatever
  its type.
</ParamField>

## Order and size of the results

<ParamField body="sort_by" type="string" default="relevancy">
  - `relevancy`: best match first.
  - `published_at`: newest publication first.
  - `crawled_at`: newest crawl first.

  A date order sorts the best matches of `q` (at least 100, or 5 times
  `limit`), not every article of the window. With a date order,
  `collapse=diverse` keeps every article, as `duplicates` does. A publication
  date later than the crawl date counts as the crawl date.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="limit" type="integer" default="20">
  Number of articles to return, 1 to 100. Your plan may allow fewer (50 on the
  free plan); a larger `limit` gets `403`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="collapse" type="string" default="diverse">
  What happens to several articles of one story: `diverse`, `story` or
  `duplicates`. See [Stories](/news/stories#repeated-articles-of-one-story).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="include" type="string | string[]">
  Enterprise fields to add to every result. Other plans get `403` with
  `enterprise_field`. See [Response fields](/news/response-fields#enterprise-fields).
</ParamField>

## Example

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.newscatcherapi.com/news-api/search" \
  -H "x-api-key: $NEWSCATCHER_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "q": "port strike",
    "search_window": "3d",
    "countries": ["US", "CA"],
    "not_sources": ["example-aggregator.com"],
    "min_newsrooms": 3,
    "collapse": "story",
    "sort_by": "published_at",
    "limit": 50
  }'
```

## Only for `/articles`

These parameters work on [`/articles`](/news/articles) only. On `/articles`,
`zh` in `languages` and `not_languages` means both `cn` and `tw`, and
`countries` and `not_countries` are ignored when `sources` is set.

<ParamField body="search_in" type="string | string[]" default="title_body">
  Where `q` is searched, at most 2 values: `title_body`, `title`, `body`,
  `summary`, or the same fields in their English translation:
  `title_translated`, `body_translated`, `title_body_translated`,
  `summary_translated`. With a translated field, each result also has
  `title_en`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="from_date" type="string">
  Start of the window: ISO 8601 (`2026-10-03`, `2026-10-03T14:00:00`, with or
  without an offset; UTC otherwise) or a phrase such as `7 days ago`. Defaults
  to midnight 7 days ago. Up to 365 days back, or your plan's history for
  `/articles`. An earlier `from_date` is cut to it, and the response says
  `"clamped": true` in `window`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="to_date" type="string">
  End of the window, in the same formats. Defaults to the moment of the
  request. Send `window.to` back as `to_date` when you
  [page through the results](/news/articles#paging-through-the-results).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="predefined_sources" type="string | string[]">
  The best-ranked publishers of a country or of the world: `top <n> <country
  code>` or `top <n> global`, for example `top 100 US`. Combined with
  `sources`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="ranked_only" type="boolean">
  Only publishers that have a traffic rank.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="from_rank, to_rank" type="integer">
  Only publishers in this range of traffic ranks, 1 to 999999 (1 is the
  best).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="word_count_min, word_count_max" type="integer">
  Only articles with this many words.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="exclude_duplicates" type="boolean" default="false">
  Leave out copies of an article that another publisher published first.
  Needs a window that starts after 2024-05-13 with `languages=en`, and after
  2025-03-17 otherwise.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="sort_by" type="string" default="published_at">
  - `published_at`: newest first.
  - `-published_at`: oldest first.
  - `relevancy`: best match first.
  - `rank`: best-ranked publisher first.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="page, page_size" type="integer" default="1, 100">
  The page to return, and up to 100 articles per page. `page` × `page_size`
  can be at most 1,000. Your plan may allow a smaller page; a larger one gets
  `403`.
</ParamField>

A blank value, the same value in a filter and its `not_` filter, or
`from_rank` greater than `to_rank` is refused with `422`.
