---
title: Writing queries
description: Ask in plain words or keywords, in any language, and exclude terms with NOT.
---

`q` is what you are looking for: a question in plain words, or keywords with
`AND`, `OR` and `NOT`. It can be in any language and up to 500 characters long.
For the boolean query of `/articles`, see
[Boolean queries for `/articles`](#boolean-queries-for-articles).

<CodeGroup>
```text Plain words
What did central banks decide about interest rates this week?
```

```text Keywords
ECB OR "Federal Reserve"
```

```text With NOT
Apple NOT iPhone
```
</CodeGroup>

## Excluding terms with NOT

Put an upper-case `NOT` before a term or a quoted phrase to drop the articles
that mention it: `Tesla recall NOT Cybertruck`, `NOT "model y"`.

- `NOT` checks the headline, the description and the summary.
- The response lists the excluded terms in `excluded`.
- `NOT` must be upper-case.

To leave out whole languages, countries or publishers, use `not_languages`,
`not_countries` and `not_sources`. See [Filters](/news/filters).

## Languages

Articles in every language are searched through their English translation, so
an English query finds German, Japanese or Arabic articles too.

If every language you ask for in `languages` is not English, the search runs in
the original language instead. Use this to search in the language itself. Languages are
[ISO 639-1 codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_639-1):

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.newscatcherapi.com/news-api/search" \
  -H "x-api-key: $NEWSCATCHER_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"q": "Bundestag Haushalt", "languages": ["de"]}'
```

`zh` is searched as `cn` (simplified Chinese). Traditional Chinese is `tw`.

## Boolean queries for `/articles`

[`/articles`](/news/articles) takes a boolean query only, up to 1,000
characters, searched in the fields of
[`search_in`](/news/filters#only-for-articles):

| Syntax | Example | Matches |
|---|---|---|
| `AND`, `OR`, `NOT` | `Tesla AND recall NOT Cybertruck` | Articles with both words, without the third |
| Parentheses | `"interest rate" AND (cut OR hike)` | Groups of terms |
| `"exact phrase"` | `"Silicon Valley Bank"` | The words in this order |
| `+must`, `-mustnot` | `+Tesla -Musk` | Articles that must have, or must not have, a word |
| Wildcards | `regulat*` | `regulator`, `regulation`, `regulatory` |
| `NEAR` | `NEAR("rate", "decision", 5)` | The words within 5 words of each other |

A query that is only a wildcard, such as `*`, is refused with `422`.

## Tips

- Say what happened, not only who: `Nvidia export restrictions China` finds
  more of what you want than `Nvidia`.
- Use quotes for exact names and phrases: `"Silicon Valley Bank"`.
- Put the time window in `search_window`, not in `q`.
- Narrow with [filters](/news/filters) instead of long keyword lists.
