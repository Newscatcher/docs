`/search` returns the best articles for a question. `/articles` returns every
article that matches a boolean query, newest first, for you to page through.
It covers the last 12 months and reaches up to the first 1,000 articles of a
query.

## Your first request

Send the parameters as a JSON body to `POST /articles`:

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.newscatcherapi.com/news-api/articles" \
  -H "x-api-key: $NEWSCATCHER_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "q": "\"interest rate\" AND (cut OR hike) NOT crypto",
    "from_date": "2026-10-03",
    "page_size": 100
  }'
```

`GET /articles` takes the same parameters in the query string. In both, list
parameters take a comma-separated string; in the JSON body they can also be
arrays.

A successful call costs 5 NCC, whatever the page size or the number of
results, also when nothing matches. A refused or failed request is not
charged.

## Query

<ParamField body="q" type="string" required>
  The boolean query, 1 to 1,000 characters. It takes `AND`, `OR`, `NOT`,
  parentheses, `"exact phrases"`, `+must`, `-mustnot`, `wild*cards` and
  `NEAR("a", "b", 5)` (the words within 5 of each other). For example
  `"interest rate" AND (cut OR hike) NOT crypto`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="search_in" type="string | string[]" default="title_body">
  Where `q` is searched, at most 2 values: `title`, `body`, `title_body` or
  `summary`, or the same on the English translation: `title_translated`,
  `body_translated`, `title_body_translated` or `summary_translated`. A match
  in any of them counts.
</ParamField>

A query with an unclosed quote or another syntax error gets `422`, with a
message that names the problem.

## Time window

<ParamField body="from_date" type="string">
  Start of the window: ISO 8601 (`2026-10-03`, `2026-10-03T14:00:00`, with or
  without an offset; UTC otherwise) or a phrase such as `7 days ago`.
  Default: midnight 7 days ago. Up to 365 days back, or your plan's history if
  it is shorter. An earlier start is cut to it, and the response says
  `"clamped": true` in `window`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="to_date" type="string">
  End of the window, in the same formats. Default: the moment of the request.
  The response returns it in `window.to`.
</ParamField>

## Filters

<ParamField body="languages" type="string | string[]">
  Only articles in these languages: ISO 639-1 codes, at most 100. `zh` means
  `cn` (simplified) and `tw` (traditional).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="not_languages" type="string | string[]">
  Leave out articles in these languages: ISO 639-1 codes, at most 100.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="countries" type="string | string[]">
  Only articles from publishers in these countries: ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 codes,
  at most 100. Ignored when `sources` is set.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="not_countries" type="string | string[]">
  Leave out articles from publishers in these countries: ISO 3166-1 alpha-2
  codes, at most 100. Ignored when `sources` is set.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="sources" type="string | string[]">
  Only these publishers: domains, at most 100, for example
  `["reuters.com", "bbc.co.uk"]`. A domain with a subdomain
  (`news.bbc.co.uk`) matches that subdomain only.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="not_sources" type="string | string[]">
  Leave out these publishers: domains, at most 100. Only bare domains
  (`cnn.com`) leave articles out; a list of subdomains only
  (`edition.cnn.com`) excludes nothing.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="predefined_sources" type="string | string[]">
  Only the best-ranked publishers of a country or of the world:
  `top <n> <country code>` or `top <n> global`, for example `top 100 US`.
  Combined with `sources`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="ranked_only" type="boolean" default="false">
  Only articles from publishers that have a rank.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="from_rank" type="integer">
  Only publishers ranked at this position or lower (a larger number), 1 to
  999999. The rank is the publisher's traffic rank, 1 being the best. Combined
  with `sources`, an article that matches either one passes.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="to_rank" type="integer">
  Only publishers ranked at this position or better (a smaller number), 1 to
  999999. Must not be lower than `from_rank`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="word_count_min" type="integer">
  Only articles of at least this many words, 0 to 1,000,000. `0` is a filter
  too: articles without a word count are left out. Must be lower than
  `word_count_max`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="word_count_max" type="integer">
  Only articles of at most this many words, 0 to 1,000,000.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="exclude_duplicates" type="boolean" default="false">
  Leave out copies of an article that another publisher published first. Off
  by default: every match is returned. The window must start after
  2024-05-13 for English only (`languages=en`) and after 2025-03-17 otherwise.
</ParamField>

A filter can't both include and exclude the same value, for example
`languages=en` with `not_languages=en`: the request gets `422`. So does a blank
value in a list. An empty array is no filter.

## Sorting and paging

<ParamField body="sort_by" type="string" default="published_at">
  `published_at` newest first, `-published_at` oldest first, `relevancy` best
  match first, or `rank` best-ranked publisher first.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="page" type="integer" default="1">
  Page number, from 1.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="page_size" type="integer" default="100">
  Articles per page, 1 to 100. Your plan may allow fewer: a larger page gets
  `403`.
</ParamField>

A query reaches its first 1,000 articles: `page` x `page_size` is at most
1,000, and a deeper page gets `422`. To read more, narrow the query or split
the window.

<Note>
  `to_date` defaults to the moment of each request, so new articles can shift
  the pages while you read them. Send the `window.to` of the first page back
  as `to_date` on every next page, so all pages cover the same articles.
</Note>

## What comes back

```json
{
  "query": "\"interest rate\" AND cut",
  "search_in": ["title_body"],
  "sort_by": "published_at",
  "took_ms": 412,
  "window": {
    "from": "2026-10-03T00:00:00+00:00",
    "to": "2026-11-02T16:00:00+00:00"
  },
  "page": 1,
  "page_size": 100,
  "total_hits": 4812,
  "total_pages": 10,
  "count": 1,
  "results": [
    {
      "id": "219ba6eb51d067e58b0941c8f07cfc1d",
      "title": "Fed officials signal patience on further rate cuts",
      "url": "https://www.reuters.com/markets/us/fed-officials-signal-patience-further-rate-cuts-2026-11-02/",
      "source": { "domain": "reuters.com", "name": "Reuters" },
      "published_at": "2026-11-02T14:05:00+00:00",
      "author": "Howard Schneider",
      "language": "en",
      "country": "US",
      "rank": 104,
      "image_url": "https://www.reuters.com/resizer/photo.jpg",
      "snippet": "Federal Reserve officials on Monday said they were in no hurry to lower interest rates again, pointing to a labor market that remains firm and inflation that has…",
      "word_count": 812,
      "score": 12.4
    }
  ]
}
```

| Field | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `query`, `search_in`, `sort_by` | The query as received, where it was searched and the order of the results. |
| `window` | `from` and `to` of the window that was searched (UTC, ISO 8601), and `clamped: true` when `from_date` was cut to your plan's history. |
| `page`, `page_size` | The page of this response and its size. |
| `total_hits` | The exact number of articles that match in the window. A query still returns only its first 1,000. |
| `total_pages` | The pages you can read: `min(total_hits, 1000)` divided by `page_size`, rounded up. |
| `count` | The number of articles on this page. `0`, with empty `results`, when nothing matched. |
| `took_ms` | How long the search took on our side, in milliseconds. |

Each result has:

| Field | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `id` | Article ID. |
| `title` | Headline as published. |
| `title_en` | English translation of the headline, only when `search_in` has a translated field. |
| `url` | Link to the article. |
| `source` | The publisher: `domain` and `name`. |
| `published_at` | When the article was published (UTC, ISO 8601). |
| `author` | The author, as published. |
| `language` | ISO 639-1 code. Chinese is `cn` (simplified) or `tw` (traditional). |
| `country` | Country of the publisher, ISO 3166-1 alpha-2. |
| `rank` | Traffic rank of the publisher: 1 is the best, 999999 means no rank. |
| `image_url` | Link to the main image of the article. |
| `snippet` | Start of the publisher's description, cut at a word boundary to your plan's snippet length (200 characters by default). |
| `word_count` | Number of words of the article. |
| `score` | Relevance to `q`. Only the order means something, not the value. |

A field without a value is left out of the result.

## Limits and errors

Your plan sets the largest `page_size`, how many pages you can read, how far
back you can search and the requests per second for `/articles`. See them,
with the price of a call, in the `articles` field of
[`GET /subscription`](/get-started/rate-limits#check-your-plan).

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| `403` | A larger page or a deeper page than your plan allows, a window that ends before your plan's history, or a filter of the Enterprise plans (`enterprise_field`). |
| `408` | The search ran out of time and was stopped. Retry with a narrower window, a smaller `page_size` or a simpler query. |
| `422` | A parameter is missing, invalid or not a parameter of `/articles`, the query is not valid, or `page` x `page_size` is over 1,000. For an unknown parameter, the message lists the allowed ones. |

None of these is charged. See [Errors](/get-started/errors) for the other
statuses.
