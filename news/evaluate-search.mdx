The best test of a search API is your own queries. This method takes a day
and gives you numbers you can compare across effort levels and providers.

## 1. Collect real queries

Take 50 to 100 queries from your logs or your users, across the topics and
languages you care about. Avoid queries you wrote to make one provider look
good.

## 2. Run every query at each effort level

Run each query at `low`, `medium` and `high` with the same `window_hours`, and
store the top 10 results.

## 3. Judge relevance

For each result, ask one question: does this article answer the query? Judge
one result at a time, with a person or an LLM judge, and check a sample of the
LLM verdicts by hand.

## 4. Compare

- **Precision at 10**: the share of the top 10 results that are relevant.
- **Distinct events in the top 10**: how many different stories the results
  cover.
- **Freshness**: how old the relevant results are.
- **Latency and cost** per query.

## 5. Compare with other providers

Run the same queries, with the same time window, through each provider and
judge them the same way.
