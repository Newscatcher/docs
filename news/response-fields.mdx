## Search response

<ResponseField name="query" type="string">
  The query as received, with control characters replaced and the ends trimmed.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="effort" type="string">
  The effort that ran: `low`, `medium` or `high`.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="sort_by" type="string">
  The order of the results: `relevancy` (best match first), `published_at` or
  `crawled_at` (newest first).
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="took_ms" type="integer">
  How long the search took on our side, in milliseconds.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="window" type="object">
  The time window that was searched.
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="search_window" type="string">Length of the window as a `search_window` value (`7d`, `2h`, `15m`): the one you sent, your plan's limit when it was cut, or `7d` by default.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="hours" type="number">The same length in hours, a fraction for windows in minutes.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="from" type="string">Start of the window: `to` minus its length (UTC, ISO 8601).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="to" type="string">End of the window: when the search ran (UTC, ISO 8601).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="clamped" type="boolean">True when the window you asked for was longer than your plan allows and was cut. Absent otherwise.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="count" type="integer">
  Number of results returned, at most `limit`.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="results" type="object[]">
  The articles, in the order of `sort_by`. See below.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="excluded" type="string[]">
  The terms of the `NOT` clauses of `q`. Absent when `q` has no `NOT`.
</ResponseField>

## Articles response

The response of [`/articles`](/news/articles).

<ResponseField name="query" type="string">
  The query as received, with control characters replaced and the ends trimmed.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="search_in" type="string[]">Where `q` was searched.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="sort_by" type="string">The order of the results.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="took_ms" type="integer">
  How long the search took on our side, in milliseconds.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="window" type="object">
  The time window that was searched.
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="from" type="string">Start of the window (UTC, ISO 8601): your `from_date`, or your plan's history limit when it cut it.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="to" type="string">End of the window (UTC, ISO 8601): your `to_date`, or the moment of the request. Send it back as `to_date` when paging.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="clamped" type="boolean">True when `from_date` was earlier than your plan allows and was cut. Absent otherwise.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="page" type="integer">The page of this response.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="page_size" type="integer">The page size of this response.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="total_hits" type="integer">
  The exact number of articles that match in the window.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="total_pages" type="integer">
  The pages you can read: the first 1,000 matches divided by `page_size`,
  rounded up.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="count" type="integer">
  The number of articles on this page. `0` when nothing matched.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="results" type="object[]">
  The articles, in the order of `sort_by`. Each has `id`, `title`, `url`,
  `source`, `published_at`, `language`, `country`, `snippet` and `score`, as
  in [Article](#article), plus:
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="title_en" type="string">English translation of the headline. Only when `search_in` searches a translated field.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="author" type="string">The author, as published.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="rank" type="integer">The publisher's traffic rank: 1 is the best. `999999` means the publisher has no rank.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="image_url" type="string">Link to the main image of the article.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="word_count" type="integer">Number of words of the article.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Article

<ResponseField name="id" type="string">Article id.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="title" type="string">Headline as published.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="title_en" type="string">English translation of the headline. Non-English articles only.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="summary" type="string">
  One-line summary in the article's language. Absent when the article has none.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="summary_en" type="string">
  One-line summary in English. Non-English articles only.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="snippet" type="string">
  Start of the publisher's description, up to your plan's snippet length (200
  characters on the free plan).
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="url" type="string">Link to the article.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="source" type="object">
  The publisher: `domain` (without `www.`) and `name`.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="language" type="string">
  ISO 639-1 code. Chinese is `cn` (simplified) or `tw` (traditional).
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="country" type="string">Country of the publisher, ISO 3166-1 alpha-2.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="published_at" type="string">When the article was published (UTC, ISO 8601).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="crawled_at" type="string">When NewsCatcher crawled it (UTC, ISO 8601).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="content_type" type="string">The type of content the article was classified as, for example `news_report`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="story" type="object">
  The [story](/news/stories) it belongs to: `id` and `newsrooms`.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="score" type="number">
  Ranking score. Only the order of the results means something, not the value.
</ResponseField>

## Enterprise fields

Enterprise plans can add heavy fields to every result with `include`. Other
plans get `403` with `enterprise_field`. Your plan's fields are in
`extra_fields` of `GET /subscription`. For everything else Enterprise plans
add, see [Enterprise plans](/get-started/enterprise-plans).

| `include` | Field | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| `description` | `description` | The description the publisher wrote for the article. |
| `entities` | `entities` | People (`ner_PER`), organizations (`ner_ORG`), locations (`ner_LOC`) and other names (`ner_MISC`), plus the same found in the English translation. |
| `sentiment` | `sentiment` | Sentiment of the headline and of the article, from -1 (very negative) to 1 (very positive). |
| `themes` | `themes` | Themes of the article. |
| `translation` | `translation` | English headline and summary of a non-English article. |
| `embedding` | `embedding` | A 1,024-value vector of the article, rounded to 4 decimal places. |
